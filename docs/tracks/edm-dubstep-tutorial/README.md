# EDM / dubstep tutorial track (glyphcore/edm)

A 104-bar, 140 bpm half-time dubstep track (178 s) that teaches itself: each section is one lesson, written as comments above the
lanes that do it. The format follows the narrated "dubstep tutorial" tracks of the early 2010s (Dubba Jonny for UKF: a voice gives a
production tip and the music then does it; what we know about those videos and what is UNVERIFIED is in docs/EDM_REFERENCES.md). The
lessons are our own, all sound is synthesised (no samples), CC0-1.0. Issue #129; components #127 (edm-core) and #128 (dubstep).

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `track.strudel.js` | the Craft program (source of truth): pack imports, atoms (`ec.`, `ds.`), racks as `.hook("...")`, section masks |
| `track.standalone.strudel.js` | GENERATED by `node tools/concepts/build.mjs` (the lowering, docs/LOWERING.md): plain Strudel, paste into strudel.cc |
| `form.json` | the form for `engine/genre/score.py --form` (suno-track-lab) |
| Recipes view | "Dubstep tutorial track (104 bars, 11 lessons)": Craft and Strudel tabs |

## Arrangement map (bars, lessons, techniques)
Gates and masks are the edm-core 104-bar map (`ec.intro` .. `ec.outro`, masks `ec.mIntro` .. `ec.mOutro`; docs/EDM_REFERENCES.md section 3).

| bars | section | lesson | lanes | technique taught |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-16 | intro | 1, 2 | pad; shaker and kick on the 1 from bar 9; vowel chops bars 13-16 | key first; filter automation as data (`.lpf("<500 .. 1700>")`); rhythm before weight |
| 17-24 | build | 3 | 8-bar snare roll (`ec.roll8`, `ec.rollVel8`), chord stabs with `ec.open8` / `ec.thin8`, `noiseRiser(13.71)`, gap on the last beat | tension = density (roll: 4, 4, 8, 8, 16, 16, 16, 32 hits per bar) + brightness + level, then the breath; kick pulse, shaker and a filtered growl pre-echo (bars 21-24) add layers |
| 25 | drop A lands | 4 | `impactBoom`, `crash`, the kit | mark the downbeat in three bands |
| 25-40 | drop A | 5, 6 | half-time kick / snare / ghosts / 8th hats; `subSine` + `growlBass` (vowel `<a o>`: a in one bar, o in the next, not per note) / `talkBass` call and response (`ds.bassA` / `ds.bassB`); `laserZap` in every 4th bar | sidechain (`duckorbit("2:3")`, depth 0.36 / 0.5 = -8 / -10.7 dB); band split by layering |
| 41-56 | breakdown | 7 | `downSweep`; `ukfChord` pumped by a ghost trigger (silent kick, `duckorbit(3)`); `ukfArp`; `ukfLead` from 49; chops 49-56 | contrast; pump with no audible trigger; melody |
| 57-64 | build 2 | 8 | roll + filter stabs again; `hpBuild(13.71)` + `pitchRiser(13.71)`; `reverseSwell(1.71)` into bar 65 | build differently the second time |
| 65-76 | drop B | 9 | `wobbleBass` with `lpsync` as a pattern (`ds.wobbleSync`), 16th hats, ducked `ukfChord` | the second drop changes something: synced filter LFO |
| 77-88 | switch-up | 10 | `riddimBass` (3 + 3 + 2), `screechLead` in the gaps, crash on 77 | same kit, new idea |
| 89-104 | outro | 11 | drums, hats and shaker for 8 bars at half level, fading as postgain data (`FADE`, `SHAKE`: 0.5 -> 0.08); filtered growl (89-92) and talk (93-96) callbacks; then only the pad, fading over 97-104 as velocity data; `downSweep` on 89 | leave 16 bars for the next DJ; master last |

Render budget: the track is written to render headless in reasonable time. Measured per bar (RACK_STANDARD section 9) the AudioWorklet
stages cost 15-45x a native voice in the CLI, so the chord, pad and lead lanes use `WIDE` (three native saws 0.15 semitone apart, panned:
what a supersaw is) after their racks, the breakdown pump is a ghost trigger (a silent four-on-the-floor with `duckorbit(3)`) instead of the
`lfoPump` tremolo, and drums, sub, wobble and riddim drop the racks' `shape` stage (`.withValue(dry)`, which deletes `shape` / `distort` from each event), the talk bass its `distort`. The
growl and screech keep their drive and the talk and wobble their filter LFOs: those are the lessons. On the site the full racks play as they are.

Orbits (Strudel buses): 1 drums and FX (never ducked), 2 bass layers (ducked -8 dB, back in 150 ms), 3 music (ducked -10.7 dB, back
in 300 ms, the only orbit with a room). superdough maps duck depth d to a gain of 1 - sqrt(d) (read in `superdoughoutput.mjs`).

pull the integrated loudness down, the master's gain follows the drops, and the limiter keeps the reliefs below the drops instead of
