# Sirocco Foundry (house into wet industrial dubstep, C minor, 140 bpm, 128 bars = 3:39)

Issue #136. A full-length piece: big-room Italo / French house energy for the first half, a wet industrial halftime drop in the middle, both worlds in
the return. Feel and production approach only: no melody, sample or arrangement of an existing song, no words (vowels only, the repo's own vox bank
glyphcore/vox `vocalset`). Everything below is **UNLISTENED**: nobody has heard any element; the track was written against measurements.

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `track.strudel.js` | the Craft form (source of truth): atoms, local racks (`hooks.def`), lanes, the arrangement as `tagged("#lane").mute(gate)` |
| `track.standalone.strudel.js` | GENERATED by `node docs/tracks/sirocco-foundry/build.mjs`: plain Strudel (atoms inlined, racks written out, gates as `.mask("<...>")`), paste into strudel.cc |
| `form.json` | the form for the section measurement (`measure_sections.mjs`) |
| `build.mjs` | the arrangement table `ON` (lane -> bars), writes the gates block of the Craft form and the standalone through `tools/lowering/lower.mjs` |
| `measure_sections.mjs` | static gain to -14 LUFS, whole-track and bar-aligned per-section measurement (ffmpeg + `tools/racks/measure.mjs`) |
| `measure.json` | committed measurements of the delivered v2 render, the standalone render and the spread |
| `build.mjs --check` | fails when the committed standalone differs from a fresh build |

## Build, render, measure
```
node docs/tracks/sirocco-foundry/build.mjs                                   # gates + standalone
node tools/racks/render_track.cjs docs/tracks/sirocco-foundry/track.strudel.js OUT.wav --bars 1-128 --tail 4    # one pass, 3 min
node docs/tracks/sirocco-foundry/measure_sections.mjs OUT.wav measure.json --workdir DIR
node tools/racks/measure.mjs --bpm 140 --score docs/genres/house.json DIR/s_drop1.wav
```
The full 128 bars plus 4 tail bars render in **one pass** (about 2.5 to 3 minutes, 0 failed events, 7308 haps); no per-section joins. The static gain is
one `volume=` per file (about +10.6 dB) with no limiter and no compression after the render; the MP3 is 256 kbit/s from the normalised float WAV
(decoded: -14.0 LUFS, -1.22 dBTP). Audio is not committed.

## Harmony, motif, voices
- **Key and loop.** C minor. House loop of 8 bars: Cm - Ab - Eb - Bb - Cm - Ab - Fm - G (the G pulls into the Cm of the next pass); voicings move by step
  (top voice c5 - c5 - d5 - c5 - c5 - c5 - c5 - g4; the Bb voicing is d4 f4 bb4 c5, so only d4 and f4 move to eb4 and g4 for the Cm). Drop 2 stays on C with a Db pull in bar 4 of every 4.
- **One motif.** The owner's seed rhythm `[c2@3 c2] [~ eb2] [c2@2 g1@2] ~` is the dubstep growl (atom `sfDrop`), and the same rhythm moved onto the 8-bar
  roots is the house bassline (`sfSeed`): the two halves share the motif. The seed voice is kept as given in the hook `seedGrowl` (supersaw, fm 2.57,
  fmh 7.82, vowel a, hpf 189, lpf 619, lpsync 4, lpdepth 1.02, attack .005, sustain .63, release .258, distort 1.37, distortvol .665, gain .26), with a
  `shape`/`crush` serial stage and a compressor (peak control) on the lanes. `seedHouse` is the low-passed, mono, ducked variant (lpf 460, no vowel, spread 0) over a sine sub.
- **Hook** (vowel chop, `vs_a` for bars 1-4 of each 8 and `vs_o` for 5-8, chord tones on the strong 16ths) is stated whole at 41, embellished at 49 (hook B), answered in fragments in
  drop 2, returns in drop 3, pre-echoed in the intro (13-16) and the breakdown (69-72) and called back in the outro (121-124), following the foreshadow / callback idea in docs/COMPOSITION.md (FS family).
- **Buses (orbits):** 1 drums and FX (never ducked), 2 bass (kick-ducked), 3 music with a room (ducked), 4 the wet industrial bus (own reverb, ducked).
  The kick carries `duckorbit("2:3:4")`. Section gates are `tagged("#lane").mute("...")` ranges (complement of the lane's bars).

## Techniques used / not used (design deviations, stated)
Used: atoms (`atoms.import` of glyphcore/edm plus local `atom()` mini patterns), gates as arrangement, local racks via `hooks.def` and `.hook`,
`.groove` on the open hat and the metal percussion (the closed hats use an explicit `.swingBy(0.14, 8)`, see Measurements), kick-keyed sidechain (`duckorbit`), buses by orbit, vox sample bank, rack library (`edmKick`, `dsKick`, `superChord`, `atmosPad`,
`ukfPluck`, `ukfArp`, `screechLead`, `noiseRiser`, `hpBuild`, `pitchRiser`, `reverseSwell`, `downSweep`, `impactBoom`, `crash`, ...).
Not used, with the reason: `.tap`/`.wire`, `.send`/`ret` buses and the `dt.*` dovetail gate atoms (gated `hooks.bind`) have no plain-Strudel form (docs/LOWERING.md),
and the issue wants a paste-ready standalone with matching haps; dovetails are therefore written as lanes (tail, pickup, reverse, impact) with masks. The `tagged(...).mute` gates
and the vox sample lanes are Craft-only and are lowered to masks and synthesised twins (`vowelChop`, a saw pad) in the standalone (marked `//@craft` / `//@plain` in the source).
