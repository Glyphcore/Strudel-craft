# Velvet Static (#137)

A full-length (3:41) sentimental liquid drum and bass track with night-phonk floats, written as Strudel-craft syntax. 174 bpm throughout
(`setcpm(174 / 4)`, one cycle = one 4/4 bar), F minor, 160 bars. No lyrics; nothing is copied from a song. **Nobody has listened to any of
it.** Every element below is marked "unlistened"; what is known about it is a measurement, not a judgement of taste.

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `track.strudel.js` | the Craft program, source of truth: atoms, `hooks.def` racks bound into slots, lanes, section gates written as data |
| `track.standalone.strudel.js` | GENERATED by `node docs/tracks/velvet-static/lower.mjs` (repo lowering, docs/LOWERING.md; `--check` for staleness): atoms inlined, racks and slots written out as plain chain steps, `.groove()` as `.swingBy / .late / .mul(velocity(rand))`. Paste into the site. The two `voxBank(...)` calls stay (site vox prelude) |
| `form.json` | the 8 sections, in bars |
| `measure.json` | every number quoted here (whole track, sections, genre score, stems, duck, takes, rounds) |
| `lower.mjs` | the generator of the standalone (it hides `voxBank` from the lowering's classifier while it runs) |

Audio is never committed. Render and files: see "Render" at the end.

## Music (written for this repo)
- **Harmony.** i VI III VII = Fm9, Dbmaj9, Abmaj9, Ebmaj7, two bars each. The pad voicings are `[eb g ab c] [eb f ab c] [eb g bb c] [eb g bb d]`: Eb4 is a common tone of all four chords (a pedal inside the pad), and each chord moves by one or two semitones from the last (Eb to Fm9: bb to ab, d to c). Nothing sits below C4 (no thirds under 200 Hz); the roots live in the sub. The float uses the same four voicings rotated to Ab I, Eb V, Fm vi, Db IV: the "lift" is a change of centre, not new notes. The last lift is a whole tone up (Gm, `add(note("<0!96 2!64>"))` on every main lane). The way in:
  Fm9 `[eb g ab c]` to Ebmaj7 `[eb g bb d]` to **D7 `[f# a c d]`** (every voice a semitone or a tone down or up) to Gm9 `[f a bb d]` (f# to f, c to bb): the leading note resolves in the pad.
- **Motif.** One rhythm, `[x@3 x] [~ x] [x@2 x@2] ~` (atom `vs.motif`), is the first bar of the lead (`c5 eb5 . f5 eb5 c5`), the snarl's rhythm (the owner's seed pattern), and the pickup rhythm of the syllable chop (the chop plays the lead melody an octave down on `la na da la`).
  The 8-bar lead phrase answers itself (bar 5 repeats bar 1 with g5 for f5). Foreshadowing and return (docs/COMPOSITION.md FS-01, FS-06, FS-05, MOT-05): a filtered fragment in build 1 (`linger(0.25)`), the full statement in the intro, a changed melody in the float, the return in drop 2 a tone up, and the augmented callback in the outro.
- **Drums.** Two-step with ghosts (kick on 1 and the "and" of 3; second pattern from 49 with a kick on step 7; snare on 2 and 4; 4-bar ghost-snare cycles with a denser one in drop 2; shuffled 16th hats at `swing 56`; off-beat open hat); halftime floats (kick, snare on 3, 8th hats with phonk-style stutter bars). Every 8th bar of a drop has a fill, every boundary has a gap, a swell, an impact or a sweep.
- **Sound ("creamy").** Warm pad (supersaw, 5 to 6 unison, low-pass 1.7 to 2.6 kHz, soft saturation), lead low-pass at most 8 kHz, hats shelved at 11 kHz, sub sine below the snarl. Vox: VocalSet vowels for the pads, the `sung` bank's `la na da` for the chops.

## Techniques from this repo
Atoms (`vs.*`, plus `ec.roll8 / rollVel8 / open8` from glyphcore/edm and `ph2.heart / rim / stut` from glyphcore/phonk, imported with `atoms.import`); `hooks.def` racks that each begin at a hygiene class, bound with `hooks.bind` and used by `.use("slot")` (they lower to plain chains); `.groove({swing, jitter, vel, seed})`; bus structure by orbit (1 drums, 2 bass, 3 music, 4 snare room, 5 fx) with the **kick-keyed sidechain** `duckorbit("2:3")`, `duckdepth("0.2:0.08")`, `duckattack("0.12:0.2")`; forms as data gates (`on(...)`, `hole(...)`, `fall(...)`, `fade(...)` build `<0!32 1!32 ...>` strings from the section table at the top of the file); two `voxBank` calls.
**Not used, and why (decisions that differ from the issue's list):** `.tap / .wire`, lane tags and `tagged(...).mute`, `.send / ret` have no plain-Strudel form (docs/LOWERING.md section 3), so they would have made the standalone differ from the Craft form; the same motif is shared through one atom instead of a tap. Gated `hooks.bind` and the `dt.*` dovetail atoms are likewise runtime-only; dovetails are written as data (the `hole` gap, reverse swells, impacts, riser lanes, `fall` filter closes, `ec.open8`). No liquid feel from #123 exists in the repo, so the groove is local (`swing 56`, jitter 3 to 4, seed per lane). `.compressor(...)` is applied per voice by superdough, not per bus, so it cannot act as a bus limiter (tried on the drum lanes: louder, higher peaks); peak control is the mix itself (levels, per-voice `shape`).

- Whole-track PLR 13.67 dB: needed loudness at -14.9, not -14.0 (above). The genre PLR (7 to 10) is missed in the whole file and in both drops (12.5, 11.8).
- Intro (-14.2 LUFS) and bridge (-14.5) are within 0.7 LU of drop 1: they were raised to keep the whole track's PLR under the true-peak limit. The arc is carried by density (onsets 12.5 and 7.3 against 15.6) and spectrum (low share 3 % against 54 %), not by level alone. Unlistened: this may sound flat.
- Onsets per bar 15.6 in the drops (16 minimum), low-band share 39 % over the whole file (the intro, bridge and builds have none).
- Duck depth reads 5.5 dB (held bass) and 14 dB (rhythmic), both "unreliable" by the tool's own flag, so it is not scored.
- Standalone: `.groove()` lowers approximately (docs/LOWERING.md section 4); the vox banks need the site prelude, so the standalone is "plain Strudel plus voxBank", not strudel.cc-ready.
