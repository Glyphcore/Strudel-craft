# Velvet Static (#137)

A full-length (3:41) sentimental liquid drum and bass track with night-phonk floats, written as Strudel-craft syntax. 174 bpm throughout
(`setcpm(174 / 4)`, one cycle = one 4/4 bar), F minor, 160 bars. No lyrics; nothing is copied from a song. **Nobody has listened to any of
it.** Every element below is marked "unlistened"; what is known about it is a measurement, not a judgement of taste.

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `track.strudel.js` | the Craft program, source of truth: atoms, `hooks.def` racks bound into slots, lanes, section gates written as data, the bus stage |
| `track.standalone.strudel.js` | GENERATED by `node docs/tracks/velvet-static/lower.mjs` (repo lowering, docs/LOWERING.md; `--check` for staleness): atoms inlined, racks and slots written out as plain chain steps, `.groove()` as `.swingBy / .late / .mul(velocity(rand))`. Paste into the site. `voxBank(...)`, `.send(...)` and `ret(...)` have no plain form and stay as they are (site prelude and Craft lane engine), so it is "plain Strudel plus the site", not strudel.cc-ready |
| `form.json` | the 8 sections, in bars |
| `measure.json` | every number quoted here (whole track, sections, genre score, stems, duck, band energies) |
| `lower.mjs` | the generator of the standalone (hides `voxBank`, `.send`, `ret` from the lowering's classifier while it runs) |
| `check.mjs` | parity guard: fails when the committed standalone differs from a fresh build, or when Craft and standalone give different hap counts in a bar window (per-lane bisection, `.groove` lanes +-2) |

Audio is never committed. Render and files: see "Render" at the end.

## Music (written for this repo)
- **Harmony.** i VI III VII = Fm9, Dbmaj9, Abmaj9, Ebmaj7, two bars each. The pad voicings are `[eb g ab c] [eb f ab c] [eb g bb c] [eb g bb d]`: Eb4 is a common tone of all four chords (a pedal inside the pad) and each chord moves by one or two semitones. Nothing sits below Eb4 (no thirds under 200 Hz in the voicings); the roots live in the sub. The float uses the same four voicings rotated to Ab I, Eb V, Fm vi, Db IV: the "lift" is a change of centre, not new notes. The last lift is a whole tone up (Gm, `add(note("<0!96 2!64>"))` on every main lane). The way in:
  Fm9 `[eb g ab c]` to Ebmaj7 `[eb g bb d]` to **D7 `[f# a c d]`** (every voice a semitone or a tone) to Gm9 `[f a bb d]` (f# to f, c to bb): the leading note resolves in the pad.
- **Motif.** One rhythm, `[x@3 x] [~ x] [x@2 x@2] ~` (atom `vs.motif`), is the first bar of the lead (`c5 eb5 . f5 eb5 c5`), the snarl's rhythm (the owner's seed pattern), and the rhythm of the syllable chop (the chop plays the lead melody an octave down on `la na da la`).
  The 8-bar lead phrase answers itself (bar 5 repeats bar 1 with g5 for f5). Foreshadowing and return (docs/COMPOSITION.md FS-01, FS-06, FS-05, MOT-05): a filtered fragment in build 1 (`linger(0.25)`), the full statement in the intro, a changed melody in the float, the return in drop 2 a tone up, the augmented callback in the outro.
- **Drums.** Two-step with ghosts (kick on 1 and the "and" of 3; second pattern from 49 with a kick on step 7; snare on 2 and 4; 4-bar ghost-snare cycles, denser in drop 2; shuffled 16th hats at `swing 56`; 32nd ghost hats; off-beat open hat); halftime floats (kick, snare on 3, 8th hats with phonk-style stutter bars). 8-bar variations: every 8th bar of a drop has a snare fill and a 32nd hat run, bar 49 changes the kick and the lead; every boundary has a gap, a swell, an impact or a sweep.
- **Sound ("creamy").** Warm pad (supersaw, 5 to 6 unison, low-pass 1.7 to 2.6 kHz, soft saturation `shape 0.4`), lead low-pass at most 8 kHz, hats shelved at 11 kHz, sub sine below the snarl. Vox: VocalSet vowels for the pads, the `sung` bank's `la na da` for the chops.

## Techniques from this repo
Atoms (`vs.*`, plus `ec.roll8 / rollVel8 / open8` from glyphcore/edm and `ph2.heart / rim / stut` from glyphcore/phonk, imported with `atoms.import`); `hooks.def` racks that each begin at a hygiene class, bound with `hooks.bind` and used by `.use("slot")`; `.groove({swing, jitter, vel, seed})`; forms as data gates (`on(...)`, `hole(...)`, `fall(...)`, `lv(...)` build `<0!32 1!32 ...>` strings from the section table at the top of the file); lanes and buses (`.dry(0).send("master" | "bus2", 1)` and `ret`); the kick-keyed sidechain `duckorbit`; two `voxBank` calls.
**Not used:** `.tap / .wire` (a wire duck did not move the held-bass test in this render harness, 0.9 dB with or without it) and tags (no plain form); the motif is shared through one atom. No liquid feel (#123) exists, so the groove is local. Dovetails are data (the `hole` gap, reverse swells, impacts, riser lanes, `fall`, `ec.open8`), not the gated `dt.*` binds.

### The bus stage (the track's own limiter, in Strudel)
`.compressor(...)` on a voice is per voice; the compressor on a return (`ret`) works on the sum of the lanes sent to it. Two returns: **`master`** takes every lane on orbits 1, 3, 4, 5 (drums, music, snare room, fx), **`bus2`** takes the bass (sub, snarl) and sits on orbit 2. Threshold -8 dB, ratio 20, no knee, attack 1 ms (the browser's compressor looks ahead about 6 ms), release 80 ms; the browser adds its own make-up gain, so the stage lifts the quiet parts a little and squashes only the peaks over -8. No limiter touches the file.
What was tried (bars 49-56 and 105-112 unless a whole render is named): per-voice compressor on kick or pad, lead, chop, keys, arp: crest 11.0 to 10.3 dB, no use. One return per orbit (5 returns): works with the duck (the return sits on the ducked orbit: held duck 10 dB at depth 0.45) but the whole render peaked 12 dB over its loudness (PLR 14.95). One single `master` return for everything: whole PLR 12.7 and drops 10 to 11, but a bus bypasses the orbit gain, so the duck vanished (held duck 0.2 to 0.4 dB). Bass on its own return on orbit 2 plus the rest on `master` (the final): whole PLR 12.5 to 12.8, drops 10.0 / 9.7, held duck 4.6 dB. The music orbit is not ducked in this build (it is on `master`); the snare no longer ducks it.
