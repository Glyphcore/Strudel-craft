# glyphcore/dnb-electro: licence

The authored content of this pack (every atoms, hooks and recipes file, this README) is dedicated to the public domain under
**CC0-1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required for it.

The pack ships no audio. Its breaks are synthesised patterns in the style of well-known breakbeats (amen-style, think-style), not samples
of the recordings; a program that loads a sampled break (the Syntax example "Chop a real break") must use a break it has the rights to.
