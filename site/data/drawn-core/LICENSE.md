The instruments, recipes and manifest of `glyphcore/drawn-core` are our own work, released under CC0-1.0 (public domain dedication).

The instruments are control points and parameters computed from short formulas (a clipped sine with a second harmonic; sums of a few harmonics for three vowel-like shapes). They are not derived from, traced over or measured from any recording, and the pack contains no audio. The wavetables are built in the page when a program names a sound.
