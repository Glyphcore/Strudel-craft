# glyphcore/phonk: licence

The authored content of this pack (atoms.json, racks.json, presets.json, this README) is dedicated to the public domain under
**CC0-1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required for it.

The pack ships no audio. The track preset plays the site's vox banks (pack `glyphcore/vox`), which keep their own licences;
credit them when you publish a render that uses them:

- LJSpeech 1.1 words (`voxBank('ljspeech')`): public domain. LJ Speech Dataset, Keith Ito, https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/ (LibriVox readings).
- VocalSet vowels (`voxBank('vocalset')`): CC BY 4.0. "VocalSet: A Singing Voice Dataset", Julia Wilkins et al., https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1193957 (trimmed, faded, normalised).
- Sung words (`voxBank('sung')`): Apache-2.0 (SoulX-Singer render, Soul-AILab) and CC BY 4.0 (VocalSet female6 timbre prompt).
- Call me baby (`voxBank('call-me-baby')`): the site owner's own vocals; licence open (Glyphcore/Strudel-craft#66).
